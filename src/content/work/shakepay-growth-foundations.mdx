## Context
When launches move fast, teams need a shared definition of success and instrumentation that supports iteration.

## What I built
- An inflows North Star metric hierarchy for 1.5M+ customers, used by Product, Engineering, Marketing, Sales, and Operations for experiments and GTM.
- Experimentation standards that moved the company from full rollouts to A/B and multivariate tests, with stratified randomization and sequential vs. fixed-horizon designs.
- AI skills (Codex) that turn PM and engineering ideas into sized A/B test briefs.

## Outcomes (high-level)
- Grew quarterly inflows $4M+ after showing that slow first support replies halved deposits, which led Support to extend hours instead of hiring.
- Raised new-customer activation 3.3pp (est. +$2.4M monthly inflows) by cutting a 5-step path to first deposit to one prompt.
- Lifted top-tier loyalty retention from 60% to 67% by replacing a lagging tier goal with an actionable leading indicator.
