## Context
Growth and retention work gets easier when activation is measurable, health is observable, and teams share a consistent reporting surface.

## What I built
- A Random Forest model on usage data that identified the onboarding milestones that predict renewal.
- Inactivity thresholds from usage data that Marketing used to redesign re-engagement campaigns.
- Renewal signals that cross-functional teams turned into A/B test hypotheses.

## Outcomes (high-level)
- Lifted renewal rate 13pp; the onboarding framework was adopted by Lifecycle Marketing, Customer Success, and Sales.
- Cut churn 11% through redesigned re-engagement campaigns.
- Sized a $20M cross-sell opportunity across 10K+ accounts, showing that renewal timing and seat growth predicted expansion better than company size.
- Scaled A/B testing 5x across cross-functional teams.
